## Translation Guidelines

1. **Abbreviations**: Keep English abbreviations (IOB, COB, ISF, CR, SMB, UAM) throughout the text but provide Dutch translation in parentheses on first use per page.

2. **Technical Terms**: Terms like "Loop", "Override", "Autosens", "Autotune" are kept in English as they are proper feature names.

3. **Units**: Keep standard units (mg/dL, mmol/L, g, U) as-is for international consistency.

4. **Brand Names**: Keep brand names in English (Omnipod, Medtronic, Dana, Nightscout, Loop, etc.).

5. **UI Elements**: Translate UI element descriptions but keep exact menu names in English if they appear in the app.

6. **Consistency**: Always use the same Dutch term for each English term throughout all documentation.

7. **Readability**: Prioritize natural Dutch phrasing over literal translation.

8. **Medical Accuracy**: Ensure all medical terminology is technically correct and appropriate for healthcare context.
